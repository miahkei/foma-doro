About us.

Some students struggle with time management, and some experience short attention spans. Long study sessions can lead to burnout and feel so overwhelming that tasks go unfinished and procrastination sets in.

Our main purpose is to help students improve time management by helping them plan when to start and stop activities. Scheduled study sessions and breaks can also support students with short attention spans.

What you can do here

  • Plan out daily study sessions
  • Use the Pomodoro timer for focus and enhanced time management
  • Keep track of tasks so none are left behind
  • Stay on track with school events using the calendar
